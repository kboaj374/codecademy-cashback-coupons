## How people lose their cashback (the part deal articles never explain)

The most common TopCashback complaint isn't the rates — it's tracking failures, and most of them are self-inflicted in one of four ways.

1. **Ad blockers.** The number-one cause of lost cashback. Ad-blocking extensions can block the tracking script itself. Disable yours for the shopping session.
2. **Extension conflicts.** Running both the Rakuten and TopCashback browser extensions at once can cause one to overwrite the other's tracking, killing the cashback. If you use multiple portals, enable only the one you're actually shopping through.
3. **Detouring mid-checkout.** Once you've clicked through from the portal, go straight to checkout. Clicking away to another coupons site, or letting a different extension interject, can break the session. If you want to use a promo code, note it down first, then apply it at checkout without leaving the site.
4. **The browser extension isn't always enough.** The extension (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox) is handy — it pops up when you land on a participating store and shows the rate — but some retailers are only trackable through the main site, and the extension doesn't show every exclusion clause. For big-ticket Cyber Monday purchases, start at the portal's site directly.

One more expectation-setter: cashback doesn't appear instantly. Purchases show as "pending" within about a week and typically turn payable 30–90 days later, after the retailer's return window closes. ## Cyber Monday stacking FAQ

**When is Cyber Monday?** It's the Monday after Thanksgiving — November 30 in 2026, with Black Friday on November 27 the same week. Most deals go live the week before and many linger for several days after, but the deepest cuts on big-ticket items often don't survive the day itself.

**Can I stack cashback with coupon codes and sales?** Yes, in most cases. The cashback applies to what you spend regardless of the discount, which is exactly why the effective savings rate beats what any single coupon offers. Just avoid navigating away after activating the cashback, or the tracking can drop.

**Does Walmart price-match Amazon on Cyber Monday?** No. Walmart.com does not match competitor prices. Walmart physical stores do match the price of an identical item listed on Walmart.com — useful if you're buying in person.

**Is a higher discount ever worse than a higher cashback rate?** Yes, and this trips people up. A store with 15% off plus 4% cashback nets you more than 18% off with 0.5% cashback. Always compare the final number: (price after discount) minus (cashback), including tax and shipping.

**What if my cashback doesn't track?** Every portal has missing-transaction reports, and TopCashback is no exception — reviewer feedback on its support ranges from quick fixes to slow template replies. File a missing cashback claim with your order details; legitimate transactions are usually credited after manual review. Prevention (the four points above) beats the cure.
